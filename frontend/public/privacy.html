Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy outlines how PUA Vault handles your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account details (name, email) from registered admins who upload documents to the platform. Students browse and download anonymously — no personal data is collected from visitors.

2. How We Use Your Data

Staff account data is used for authentication and document management. Document metadata (title, course, year) is publicly visible.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Document metadata (title, course, year) is publicly visible. Your email and personal details remain private.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption and access controls to protect your data.

5. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact the PUA Vault administration.
